# Analysis of Women's Mental Health, Family Planning, and Social Beliefs using PNS Data

This repository contains scripts focused on understanding the relationship between mental health, women's choices in family planning, and their social beliefs influenced by religion. The analysis is based on data from the **PNS** (Pesquisa Nacional de Saúde), a Brazilian research survey conducted by the IBGE, which is based on the DHS (Demographic and Health Surveys).

## Purpose

The goal of this exercise was to explore how mental health affects women's decisions regarding family planning and how these decisions are influenced by their social beliefs, particularly those stemming from religious views.

## Data

- **PNS (Pesquisa Nacional de Saúde)**: Brazilian health survey data, which provides detailed information on health, social conditions, and other demographic factors, including mental health and family planning.
- Data for analysis was obtained from the [PNS website](https://www.ibge.gov.br/en/statistics/social/health/17025-national-health-survey.html).
